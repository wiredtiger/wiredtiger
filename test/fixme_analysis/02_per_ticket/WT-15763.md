## What this FIXME asks for
WiredTiger does not yet support graceful step-downs from leader to follower in the disaggregated storage configuration. Instead of reconfiguring in-place, the workaround is to close and reopen the connection entirely when switching to follower mode, which avoids internal-state corruption (particularly in eviction). Three test scenarios in test_layered27.py that apply leader-side writes before a role switch are fully commented out and disabled until WT can cleanly abandon uncommitted changes on step-down. The FIXME requests a proper investigation to enumerate all issues blocking in-place step-down and to fix them in separate tickets.

## Importance: HIGH
**Importance**: HIGH
**Reasoning**: The lack of graceful step-down forces a full connection restart on every leader→follower role change, which is operationally expensive and masks potential correctness bugs in WT's internal state (e.g., eviction carrying over dirty state). Disabled test paths in test_layered27.py mean that the data-correctness of write operations performed by a leader that subsequently steps down is not being validated at all.

## Test Coverage: PARTIAL
**Relevant tests**: test/suite/test_layered27.py, test/format/format_disagg.c

The format stress test also works around the limitation by restarting the connection. No test exercises the actual in-place reconfiguration path; the correctness of that path is entirely untested.
