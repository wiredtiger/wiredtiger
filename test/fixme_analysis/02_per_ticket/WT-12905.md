## What this FIXME asks for
Pre-fetch threads are explicitly excluded from participating in eviction via an early-return guard in `__wt_evict_app_assist_worker_check`. The FIXME notes this as a known limitation: pre-fetch threads cannot be pulled into application-thread eviction assist. The ticket implies this restriction should eventually be lifted or at least properly justified and documented with any design decisions needed to allow prefetch threads to safely participate in eviction.

## Importance: MEDIUM
**Reasoning**: Pre-fetch threads are responsible for reading ahead pages into cache. If the cache is under pressure and pre-fetch threads cannot assist with eviction, they may continue filling the cache with prefetched pages that the eviction system struggles to reclaim. This can contribute to cache pressure feedback loops. The fix matters for correctness under heavy memory pressure with prefetch enabled.
